# Workout Program

---

## Section 1: Posterior Chain Activation Circuit

- Single bell RDL x 20 seconds
- Hip bridge x 10 seconds
- Bird dog x 10 seconds
- Two-hand kettlebell swing x 15 reps

**Do 3 rounds.**
Men use 20-24kg | Women 12-16kg

### Exercises

1. **Single bell RDL (Romanian Deadlift) x 20 seconds** — Holding one kettlebell, hinge at the hips while keeping your back flat. Works hamstrings, glutes, and core stability. The single bell adds an anti-rotation demand.

2. **Hip bridge x 10 seconds** — Lying on your back, drive hips up and hold. Activates glutes and reinforces the hip extension pattern.

3. **Bird dog x 10 seconds** — On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg. A core stability drill that trains anti-rotation and anti-extension.

4. **Two-hand kettlebell swing x 15 reps** — The main power movement. An explosive hip hinge that ties together everything the first three exercises primed.

### Structure

- Perform all 4 exercises back-to-back as 1 round.
- Complete 3 rounds total.
- Rest as needed between rounds (typically minimal since the timed holds are short).

### Weight Recommendations

| | Kettlebell |
|---|---|
| Men | 20-24 kg |
| Women | 12-16 kg |

### Why It's Programmed This Way

The first three exercises are low-intensity activation drills — they "wake up" the posterior chain (hamstrings, glutes, core) and groove the hip hinge pattern. The swing then capitalizes on that activation with power output. It's a common approach in kettlebell programming: prime the movement pattern, then load it.

## Section 2: Heavy Grinding Conditioner

- Double start-stop swing x 5 reps
- Farmers carry x 30 seconds
- Hollow hold x 20 seconds

**AMRAP 15-20 minutes.**
Use your 6-8 rep double swing max.

### Exercises

1. **Double start-stop swing x 5 reps** — Kettlebell swings with two bells, and you fully stop/reset at the bottom of each rep (no continuous swinging). This eliminates the stretch reflex, forcing you to generate power from a dead position each time. Much harder than regular swings.

2. **Farmers carry x 30 seconds** — Walk while holding both kettlebells at your sides. Grip, core stability, posture, and conditioning all in one. Your heart rate stays elevated without high-impact movement.

3. **Hollow hold x 20 seconds** — Lying on your back, arms overhead, legs extended, press your lower back into the floor. Pure anterior core work that counterbalances all the posterior chain work from the swings.

### Structure

- **AMRAP** = As Many Rounds As Possible.
- Set a timer for 15-20 minutes.
- Cycle through all 3 exercises continuously.
- Track how many complete rounds you finish.
- Rest only as needed (the goal is sustained output, not max effort per set).

### Weight Selection

"Use your 6-8 rep double swing max" means pick the pair of kettlebells where you could only do about 6-8 heavy swings before form breaks down. This is a significant load — you're using a near-max weight but only doing 5 reps per round, which keeps each set submaximal while the cumulative volume over 15-20 minutes adds up.

### The Programming Logic

The start-stop swings demand power without momentum. The carries keep you under load while letting the hip hinge muscles recover. The hollow hold is active rest for everything except your core. The combination lets you sustain heavy work for a long duration without any single muscle group burning out.

Your score (total rounds) also gives you a benchmark to beat next time.

## Section 3: Ascending/Descending Ladder

- Kettlebell swing x 10 reps / Push up x 10 reps
- Swing x 20 reps / Push up x 8 reps
- Swing x 30 reps / Push up x 6 reps
- Swing x 40 reps / Push up x 4 reps
- Swing x 50 reps / Push up x 2 reps

**Men use 16-24kg | Women 8-16kg**

### The Pattern

| Set | Swings | Push-ups |
|-----|--------|----------|
| 1 | 10 | 10 |
| 2 | 20 | 8 |
| 3 | 30 | 6 |
| 4 | 40 | 4 |
| 5 | 50 | 2 |

- Swings go up by 10 each round.
- Push-ups go down by 2 each round.

**Totals: 150 swings + 30 push-ups**

### Why It Works

As the swings get brutally high (40, 50 reps), the push-ups drop to almost nothing — giving your grip, lungs, and posterior chain the main stage while your pressing muscles get a break. Early on, when the swings are easy, you're doing more push-up volume.

This is self-regulating fatigue management built into the structure. You never have two high-volume demands at the same time.

### Weight Selection

| | Kettlebell |
|---|---|
| Men | 16-24 kg |
| Women | 8-16 kg |

The range is lighter than the previous block — that's because sets of 30-50 swings with a heavy bell would destroy form. Pick a weight you can maintain crisp hip snap on set 5 at 50 reps.

### Pacing Strategy

No time cap is listed, so this is for completion — just get through it. Rest as needed, especially before the last two swing sets. Breaking the 40s and 50s into smaller clusters (e.g., 25+25) is perfectly fine as long as rest stays short.
